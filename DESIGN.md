# ClearWash Design Rules

Before changing the ClearWash interface, follow these project-specific rules alongside the existing Black, White & Burgundy design system.

## Surfaces

- **Text fields, selects, and textareas always use white backgrounds with dark text in both light and dark modes.**
  Why: consistent white fields improve readability and make editable controls immediately recognizable.

## Forms

- **Required labels use a burgundy dot before the label; never use a red asterisk.**
  Why: the dot provides a quieter, more professional required-field cue.
  <!-- 2026-11-24: "instead of red asterisk, add circle on the left side of text field name" -->

## Spacing & layout

- **Payment and transaction collections use full-width divided ledger rows. Place identity and metadata on the left, place the amount and primary action on the right, and stack these groups on narrow screens.**
  Why: the ledger structure makes financial records easier to scan while keeping the mobile layout usable without horizontal scrolling.
